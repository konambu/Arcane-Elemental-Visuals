[h1]Arcane Elemental Visuals[/h1]
A visual add-on for Arcane Magic - Wizards of Kenshi.
[list]
[*]Arcane Missile: red-orange fireballs, embers, a larger backward flame and impact fire.
[*]Earth Spike: matte branched rock, about 1.5x exposed height, dust and debris.
[*]Whirlwind: a spiral of fire, smoke and embers.
[/list]
Damage, mana, attack radius, speed and unlock conditions are unchanged.

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3784221846]Arcane Magic - Wizards of Kenshi[/url]
[url=https://github.com/BFrizzleFoShizzle/RE_Kenshi#installation]RE_Kenshi, including KenshiLib[/url] — install this runtime separately.
Load ArcaneMagic.mod before Arcane Elemental Visuals.mod. Fully restart after installation or updates.

[h2]Beta compatibility[/h2]
Inspected runtime: Kenshi 1.0.65 x64, RE_Kenshi 0.3.5, KenshiLib 0.5.0.
Original-mod/engine updates may require a compatibility update.
The fireball body can remain at a hit location. The player has confirmed the new v4 missile flame's appearance in Kenshi; subscription installation still needs end-to-end verification.
Standalone resource previews are not game screenshots; game lighting and interior masking can differ.
